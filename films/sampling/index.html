Org sampling policy: head and tail

Keep 100% of traces

Every healthy ping and routine hop lands in storage.
Cost scales with traffic × call depth

Keep a random 1%

Cheap, until an incident hits a 1-in-200 failure path.
99% of outage traces vanish

1 Policy ownerplatform team ships config
2 Must-keep floorerrors, slow p99, baseline
