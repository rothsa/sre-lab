Query and rule standards for the fleet

Without shared libraries

100 teams write 100 p99 queriesdifferent windows, broken averages, dropped le
Dashboards quietly disagree on the latency of the exact same service.

Platform rule library

Ship recording & SLO rules oncetested in CI · consistent across every dashboard
Every team reads the same pre-computed SLIs instead of reinventing PromQL.
Step 2 of 8

rate() first, then sum by

Right: rate per series first

sum by (job) (
  rate(http_requests_total[5m])
)
Handles pod counter resets cleanly

Wrong: sum before rate

rate(
  sum(http_requests_total)[5m]
)
Every pod restart looks like a spike
Step 3 of 8

Histograms: keep le, or go native

Classic histograms: rate → sum by (le) → quantile

histogram_quantile(0.99,
  sum by (job, le) (
    rate(http_request_duration_seconds_bucket[5m])
  )
)
Drop le and histogram_quantile breaks

Native histograms

One series per label setno manual _bucket explosion
Enable scrape_native_histograms once clients expose them for huge cardinality savings.
Step 4 of 8

Never average per-pod p99s

An average of percentiles is not a percentile

Pod 1 · 10 req/sp99 = 20 ms
Pod 2 · 1,000 req/sp99 = 900 ms
avg(p99) = 460 msstatistically invalid
Never average ratios or averages either: sum numerators and denominators, then divide
Step 5 of 8

Record the right expression once

Naming convention: level:metric:operations

- record: job:http_requests:rate5m
  expr: sum by (job) (rate(http_requests_total[5m]))
- record: job_le:http_request_duration_seconds_bucket:rate5m
  expr: sum by (job, le) (rate(http_request_duration_seconds_bucket[5m]))
Fast reads · every team gets the exact same p99 for checkout
