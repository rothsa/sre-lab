Step 7 of 9

Two boundary rules: B3 & public edge

1. B3 → W3C migration

Composite propagatorreads & writes both B3 and tracecontext
Drop B3 once no internal service sends B3-only and unexpected roots stop at that boundary.

2. Public edge trust boundary

Always start a fresh root tracekeep outside caller ID only as a link or attribute
Prevents external callers from forging trace IDs or injecting themselves into internal traces.
