    Taming Cardinality

    Every label value creates a new time series, and series are what you pay for in memory, storage and dollars. This explainer shows how cardinality grows, where it hurts, and the techniques that keep it in check. Step through each diagram with the buttons or your arrow keys, then read the short summary, the gotchas and the questions to think through.

    Based on the Prometheus and OpenTelemetry documentation and vendor docs where noted; each chapter links to its sources. To catch high-cardinality labels in PR review before deploy, see Telemetry PRs or try the Cardinality & Sampling Explorer.
