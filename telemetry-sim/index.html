Why this breaks across hundreds of teams

Cardinality multiplies silently: Prometheus and managed metric stores bill per unique label combination. Adding user_id or trace_id to a metric turns 100 series into millions and triggers sample_limit scrape failures. Keep IDs in wide events or structured metadata instead.
Sampling trades cost against outage visibility: Plain head sampling throws away 90% to 99% of error traces before a downstream service even fails. Hybrid tail sampling or dynamic wide-event sampling keeps ~100% of errors and slow p99s while thinning routine 200 OK traffic.
Catch cardinality bombs in PR review before deploy: Telemetry PRs is an open-source static GitHub Action (telemetry-prs/telemetry-prs@v0.2.0, block-on: high) that flags unbounded metric labels like user_id, trace_id, and req.url across OpenTelemetry and prom-client right in your pull request diff, 100% inside CI with no SaaS upload or LLM keys.
