    Engineering on an Exponential

    Engineers used to plan for ten-year timelines. Now a new frontier model ships somewhere most weeks, and measured agent capability compounds over months across many releases. Nobody serious plans only to the next release. The work is planning along the curve: building for where capability will be in six to twelve months, while keeping what must hold for a decade.

    That split is harder than it sounds, because the model isn't a box beside the system: it builds most of it. Chapter 1 separates its three roles (author, runtime, operator) and gives four tests for what can change and what must last: swap, regenerate, enforce, reverse.
