    Agentic Engineering, Drawn

    How teams run hundreds of coding agents and still ship to production: the harness around the model, context, verification agents can't game, many writers on one repo, review as the real bottleneck, sandboxes and scoped credentials, ops agents, and org-wide rollout. Step through each diagram with the buttons or your arrow keys, then use the 30-second answer, the gotchas and the quiz to make it stick.

    Grounded in Lauren Tan's pstack workflow (in Cursor's plugins repository) and in what Stripe, Spotify, Shopify, Uber and Microsoft publish about their agent fleets and incidents. Each chapter links to its sources.

    Diagrams and wording by Sally Roth. Sources are linked in each chapter; real-world examples come from The Incident Files.
