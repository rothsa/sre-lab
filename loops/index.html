    Graphs and Loops, Drawn

    "I'm not prompting my agents anymore, I'm building graphs and loops so they can build the agents for me." In August 2026 that line went viral, first credited to an unnamed Anthropic engineer and then to Boris Cherny, while Andrej Karpathy's name was attached to similar posts about graphs. Neither of them said it, but both said something close, and the idea is real: instead of typing each prompt, you write the loop or the small program that does the prompting, and the agent writes most of that for you.

    This explainer answers the practical questions. What is a loop, and what is a graph? Do you draw them or describe them? How do you get your agent to build them? Step through each diagram with the buttons or your arrow keys, then read the short summary, the gotchas and the questions to think through. Every quote is dated and linked to the person or vendor who said it.

    Diagrams and wording by Sally Roth. Sources are linked in each chapter. Companion explainers: One Agent to Hundreds, Agentic Engineering, Drawn and Engineering for the Next Agent, Drawn.
