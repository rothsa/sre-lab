    Graphs and Loops

    "I don't prompt Claude anymore. I have loops that are running." Boris Cherny said that on Acquired (aired June 2026). By August a bigger version, about graphs and loops building the agents, had gone viral, some of it with Karpathy's name on it. Neither man said that version. The idea underneath is real, though, and an org that adopts it ends up running hundreds of loops nobody prompts by hand.

    This explainer is about running those loops as a fleet: what a loop and a graph are, how teams register and review them, what every loop must declare before the platform runs it, and how you count, price and retire them. Step through each diagram with the buttons or your arrow keys. Every quote is dated and linked to the person or vendor who said it.

    Diagrams and wording by Sally Roth. Sources are linked in each chapter. Companion explainers: One Agent to Hundreds, Agentic Engineering and Engineering for the Next Agent.
