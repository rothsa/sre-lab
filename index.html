SRE Lab

A reliability-first lab: small, working SRE tools and illustrated, step-through explainers. The tools cover error budgets and burn-rate alerts, telling real outages from blips, failing over when a model provider breaks, searching real postmortems by failure mode, and judging an agent's incident work by its evidence. The explainers cover incident management, observability, Kubernetes, Linux, infrastructure as code, delivery and security, and what breaks once you run them at scale. Engineering with AI agents has its own section further down.
